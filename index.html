      What this covers vs. a full SOC workflow: Signal automates the static, email-side triage stage — header/authentication parsing and URL/domain risk scoring — which is where the majority of reported phishing emails get resolved. It does not (yet) correlate with identity/sign-in telemetry, live threat-intel APIs, or mailbox interaction logs, since those require authenticated access to a live tenant (e.g. Microsoft Graph sign-in logs, a VirusTotal/URLScan API key). That correlation step — connecting a URL click to a subsequent anomalous sign-in — is the next phase and the part I'd walk through designing in an interview.
